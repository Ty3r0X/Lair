As of writing I finished first year of computer science, I guess all readers who managed to finish this degree know how much of a time hog it is. Although I have a single failed exam on my shoulders to retake this autumn, I have engaged in several projects and summer schools in the hopes it improves my professional image in the future to potentially get hired ~(and perhaps the cool smart kids also do it so why not)~.

Little did I expect procrastination and lack of time planning foiled all these plans. This evening I have a group meeting for a project I did not even open despite promising to work on it. I figured out I'm going to be cooked, since in the meantime I found my time constantly working on this site.

Ever since I started this degree, I figured how valuable is to be efficient and to use all resources at time, which is why I decided to lend the help of LLMs to refactor my already plagiated site design. I know, I'm the worst person to ever exist, but at least what you see written here is made by a real human being :), so no dead internet content here :P. Still, the existence of deadlines and peer pressure in all fronts during a period where the mind needs to rest is awful. So, dear reader, not all opportunities to work are always worth it, having to rest your mind and having the flexibility to work on something you like is way more valuable. Take care out there, human!
